Welcome to New Vraja Dham

A bhakti-yoga meditation and spiritual community center based in Baton Rouge, Louisiana.

View Programs Contact Us

What to Expect

We cordially invite members of the Greater Baton Rouge community to join in congregational chanting (kirtan), spiritual discourses on the Bhagavad Gita, and honoring sanctified vegetarian food (prasadam) in a warm and welcoming environment.

Visit Our Center

Our regular programs include Bhagavad Gita discussions, Sacred Sundays (Sunday Love Feast), instrument lessons, bhakti-yoga outreach at LSU, and a vegan or vegetarian feast. Whether you are new to bhakti-yoga or are a long-term practitioner, we warmly welcome you to visit and connect with our community.
