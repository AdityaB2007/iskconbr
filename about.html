New Vraja Dham

New Vraja Dham is a growing spiritual community in Baton Rouge dedicated to sharing bhakti-yoga through kirtan, philosophy, prasadam, and devotional service.

Our programs are centered around spiritual growth, meaningful association, and creating a welcoming atmosphere where visitors can explore Krishna consciousness at their own pace.

About ISKCON

The International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), commonly known as the Hare Krishna movement, is a worldwide organization dedicated to the practice of bhakti-yoga, devotional service to Lord Krishna.

Founded in New York City in 1966 by His Divine Grace A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Srila Prabhupada, its teachings are rooted in ancient Vedic texts such as the Bhagavad-gita and Bhagavata Purana.

About Srila Prabhupada

Srila Prabhupada was a spiritual teacher and the founder of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON). He established a global community of devotees and helped spread the teachings of Gaudiya Vaishnavism across six continents.

Beginning his international mission at an advanced age with limited resources, he translated numerous Vedic texts into English, established temples around the world, and contributed to the spread of Krishna consciousness through his teachings, books, and devotional outreach.

What Is Bhakti-yoga?

Bhakti-yoga is the process of developing a loving relationship with Krishna through chanting, hearing spiritual wisdom, devotional service, and remembrance.

It is a practical and joyful spiritual path that can be integrated into daily life regardless of one's background or level of experience.
